About Science of Gifts

Science of Gifts is an independent editorial publication dedicated to the art, psychology, and material culture of thoughtful gift giving.

Thoughtful gifts, chosen with a little more thought.

An independent editorial publication exploring the intersection of behavioral psychology, material culture, and meaningful human connection.

Our Editorial Mission

Science of Gifts was founded on a simple observation: the internet is flooded with generic, algorithmically generated gift guides that optimize for affiliate volume rather than human joy. We believe giving a gift is one of humanity s oldest, most vulnerable rituals and it deserves real deliberation.

We take a deliberate, research-first approach to curation. Rather than compiling lists of 100 random novelty items, our editors and domain specialists spend weeks evaluating physical objects against strict criteria: tactile durability, aesthetic longevity, functional honesty, and narrative weight.

Our Guiding Philosophy

1. The Tuesday Test

If an object won t still be in active use, on a reading desk, or in a kitchen on a quiet rainy Tuesday six months from now, it does not belong in our guides.

2. Material Honesty

We favor solid brass, full-grain leather, archival paper, turned hardwoods, and high-fire ceramics over plated composites and disposable plastic electronics.

3. Periphery Gifting

When gifting for deep enthusiasts, we avoid guessing specific technical tools and instead curate objects that celebrate the romance and history of their craft.

4. Total Independence

We never accept sponsored product placements or free gifts in exchange for favorable placement. All our opinions and selections are strictly editorial.

The Editorial Team

Our contributors include historians, behavioral researchers, master artisans, and experienced cultural essayists based across London and New York. Each guide undergoes rigorous multi-stage review before publication.
