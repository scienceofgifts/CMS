Privacy Policy

How Science of Gifts handles and protects reader information.

1. Information We Collect

Science of Gifts values your privacy. We collect minimal information necessary to deliver our publication and provide newsletter subscriptions:

  • Email Addresses: If you subscribe to our newsletter ("The Sunday Curation"), we collect your email address solely to deliver requested editorial updates. You may unsubscribe at any time.
  • Aggregated Analytics: We collect standard, privacy-preserving aggregated data (such as page views and browser types) to understand how readers interact with our guides and improve the publication.
  • Local Storage: Features like your "Saved Gifts Shortlist" are stored locally on your device in browser storage and are never transmitted to or stored on our servers.

2. Cookies & External Links

When you click an affiliate link to a merchant or artisan site, third-party affiliate networks may set a temporary tracking cookie to record the referral. We do not sell your personal data to data brokers or third parties.

3. Contact Us

If you have any questions or wish to request deletion of your newsletter subscription data, contact our privacy desk at privacy@scienceofgifts.com.
