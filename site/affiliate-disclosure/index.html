Affiliate Disclosure & Editorial Independence

Learn about our affiliate relationships, reader-supported model, and strict editorial independence.

Affiliate Disclosure

How Science of Gifts funds its independent research, field tests, and editorial staff without compromising our integrity.

The Essential Summary: Science of Gifts is reader-supported. When you click through our curated links and make a purchase, we may receive a small affiliate commission from the retailer at no additional cost to you.

1. Editorial Independence

Our recommendations are created solely by our editorial staff and independent domain contributors. We do not accept sponsored product placements, paid reviews, or brand partnerships that dictate which objects appear in our guides.

A product is selected only if it satisfies our editorial criteria: material honesty, durability, functional utility, and genuine delight. If an exceptional object has no affiliate program, we feature it regardless.

2. How Affiliate Links Work

When you click on an external link to a retailer or maker (such as Bookshop.org, artisanal ateliers, or specialty merchants), a tracking cookie records that you came from Science of Gifts. If you complete a purchase, the merchant pays us a standard commission percentage.

This commission has zero impact on the price you pay; you pay the exact same price as any other customer.

3. Our Commitment to Readers

  • Quality Over Commission: We never recommend an inferior product simply because it pays a higher affiliate rate.
  • Regular Curation Audits: We regularly audit our guides to update out-of-stock items, pricing fluctuations, and maker changes.
  • Support for Independent Makers: We prioritize independent bookshops, heritage workshops, and ethical artisans whenever possible.
