We all know the person: they earn well, have refined tastes, and if they need an espresso machine or a cashmere scarf, they purchase it before you can even make a note.

The secret to gifting this personality type is shifting from utilitarian objects to narrative objects: hyper-regional foods with protected designations of origin, optical instruments with historical pedigrees, or humble everyday tools elevated through metallurgical mastery.
