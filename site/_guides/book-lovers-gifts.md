The hardest person to shop for is often the voracious reader. They maintain fifty-book wishlists, browse secondhand stores weekly, and have highly idiosyncratic tastes in translation and typography.

Instead of guessing their next title, give them objects that honor the ceremony of reading itself: ergonomic bookstands that keep heavy hardcovers open without spine creasing, amber reading lamps that preserve melatonin levels, and custom library embossers that mark their personal collections with timeless pride.
