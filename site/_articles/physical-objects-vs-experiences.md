For the past fifteen years, modern lifestyle commentary repeated a singular dogma: physical stuff makes people unhappy; experiences produce lasting joy. While avoiding disposable clutter is admirable, the wholesale dismissal of physical objects ignores the fundamental nature of material culture.

## The Totemic Power of the Everyday Object

Human memory is porous and fragile. We need sensory anchors. When an object is well-made, enduring, and connected to a giver, it functions as a physical mnemonic device that outlasts transient digital photos.

A well-crafted physical object is not consumer clutter; it is a permanent tactile anchor of human connection.
