We have been conditioned to believe that a legitimate gift must originate as a brand-new retail transaction accompanied by a barcode and gift receipt. But across human history, the most prized gifts were heirlooms, inherited tools, and books inscribed across generations.

## Provenance Over Packaging

When you give someone a book that altered your worldview especially one with your own margin annotations and a warm personal note explaining why this specific passage reminded you of them you are giving intimacy, not leftover inventory.

In gift-giving cultures ranging from indigenous potlatch traditions to the exchange of personal seals in Tokugawa Japan, the value of an object increases with its history. The scratches, the patina, and the story of who held it before all contribute to its relational resonance.
